# RB-12 - Cascading Microservice Failure

## 1. Scenario Identification
**ID:** S12 | **Category:** Application | **Severity:** P1-Critical | **Components:** fraud-detection, transaction-processor, payment-api, Redis, Aurora, Kafka.

## 2. Detection Mechanism
Page when payment success falls below 99.5%, P99 exceeds 300 ms for 5 minutes, or fraud-detection failures cause transaction success to fall toward 42%.

## 3. Impact Assessment
At 1,200 TPS, a ten-minute event affects up to 720,000 attempts. Protect ledger correctness; fraud bypass is permitted only with explicit risk approval and elevated monitoring.

## 4. Immediate Response (0-2 minutes)
1. **IC (15s):** Open P1 and freeze deployments.
2. **SRE (20s):** Run `kubectl -n paysecure get pods -l app=fraud-detection`.
3. **SRE (20s):** Run `aws cloudwatch get-metric-data --metric-data-queries file://application-health.json --region ap-south-1`.
4. **App lead (30s):** Disable unbounded retries and lower timeout budgets.
5. **Risk lead (30s):** Decide whether elevated-monitoring fraud bypass is legally and operationally acceptable.

## 5. Diagnostic Steps (2-5 minutes)
6. **Platform (20s):** Run `kubectl -n paysecure logs deployment/fraud-detection --since=5m`.
7. **Platform (20s):** Run `kubectl -n paysecure describe deployment fraud-detection`.
8. **DBA (20s):** Check Aurora connections with `aws rds describe-db-clusters --db-cluster-identifier $env:PAYSECURE_DB_CLUSTER_ID --region ap-south-1`.
9. **SRE (20s):** Check HPA with `kubectl -n paysecure get hpa`.
10. **IC (20s):** If fraud is isolated, trip its circuit breaker; if database saturation exists, shed traffic; if multiple services fail, invoke RB-01 or controlled regional routing.

## 6. Failover Procedure (5-15 minutes)
11. **App lead (30s):** Enable the approved circuit breaker and route fraud calls to a bounded fallback.
12. **Platform (30s):** Roll back the last image with `kubectl -n paysecure rollout undo deployment/fraud-detection`.
13. **Platform (30s):** Verify with `kubectl -n paysecure rollout status deployment/fraud-detection --timeout=60s`.
14. **SRE (30s):** Scale validated replicas with `kubectl -n paysecure scale deployment/fraud-detection --replicas=12`.
15. **IC (30s):** Reopen traffic only when success rate is above 99.5%, P99 below 300 ms, and risk owner approves the fraud policy.

## 7. Communication Protocol
Engineering receives dependency and circuit-breaker state. Management receives success rate, affected merchants, and fraud-control posture. Merchants receive payment-method impact. Compliance receives any temporary fraud-bypass decision and evidence.

## 8. Verification and Validation
Fraud errors fall, circuit breaker remains stable, payment synthetic passes, no duplicate ledger records occur, P99 and success rate recover, and Kafka settlement lag is normal.

## 9. Rollback Procedure
If rollback worsens errors, scale back the faulty version, keep the circuit open, restore the last known-good image, and re-run synthetic and reconciliation checks. Do not disable fraud controls without risk approval.

## 10. Post-Incident Review
Reconstruct dependency timeouts and retries, perform five-whys, review circuit-breaker thresholds, reconcile transactions, and assign owners for resilience changes.
