# RB-08 - Third-Party NPCI/UPI Outage

## 1. Scenario Identification
**ID:** S8 | **Category:** External | **Severity:** P2-High | **Components:** UPI connector, payment API, retry queue, settlement and reconciliation. AWS infrastructure is healthy.

## 2. Detection Mechanism
Page integration engineering when UPI failures exceed 5% for 2 minutes while card/net-banking synthetic tests and AWS health remain normal.

## 3. Impact Assessment
At the 250 TPS business baseline, a 10-minute outage affects up to 150,000 attempts. Mark transactions pending, never duplicate a debit, and communicate available alternative methods.

## 4. Immediate Response (0-2 minutes)
1. **IC (15s):** Open P2 and identify UPI-only scope.
2. **Integration (20s):** Run `curl --fail-with-body https://upi-health.paysecure.example/health`.
3. **SRE (20s):** Run `aws cloudwatch get-metric-data --metric-data-queries file://upi-metrics.json --region ap-south-1`.
4. **App lead (30s):** Disable aggressive retries and persist idempotency state.
5. **Merchant ops (30s):** Publish alternative card, wallet, and net-banking methods.

## 5. Diagnostic Steps (2-5 minutes)
6. **Integration (20s):** Compare NPCI response codes and timeout rates.
7. **SRE (20s):** Run `aws elbv2 describe-target-health --target-group-arn $env:PAYSECURE_TARGET_GROUP_ARN --region ap-south-1`.
8. **Platform (20s):** Run `kubectl -n paysecure get pods -l app=payment-api`.
9. **DBA (30s):** Inspect pending transaction count without exposing payment data.
10. **IC (20s):** If AWS and other methods are healthy, keep UPI queued; if all methods fail, invoke RB-01/RB-12; if NPCI declares recovery, begin controlled drain.

## 6. Failover Procedure (5-15 minutes)
11. **App lead (30s):** Set UPI connector circuit breaker open and queue new eligible attempts.
12. **SRE (30s):** Verify queue capacity and `kubectl -n paysecure get hpa`.
13. **Integration (60s):** Poll the approved NPCI status endpoint and partner notices.
14. **App lead (60s):** Replay only pending idempotent requests after three successful probes.
15. **Reconciliation (60s):** Match PaySecure status to NPCI and bank status before marking success.

## 7. Communication Protocol
Engineering gets response-code and queue status. Management gets pending count and merchant impact. Merchants receive update frequency and alternative methods. NPCI receives technical evidence through the partner channel; regulators are notified if SLA or settlement obligations are breached.

## 8. Verification and Validation
UPI probes succeed three times, queue depth declines, duplicate idempotency keys remain zero, card tests remain healthy, and reconciliation has no unknown debit.

## 9. Rollback Procedure
If recovery probes fail, reopen the circuit breaker, stop replay, retain pending state, and continue status updates. Never mark pending transactions successful based only on timeout recovery.

## 10. Post-Incident Review
Reconcile all pending requests, calculate customer and merchant impact, review retry storms and queue capacity, and update partner outage controls.
