# RB-02 - Controlled Regional Failback

## 1. Scenario Identification

- **ID:** S2-FB
- **Category:** Infrastructure and Data
- **Severity:** P1-Critical
- **Current production:** Hyderabad (`ap-south-2`)
- **Target production:** Mumbai (`ap-south-1`)
- **Affected components:** Aurora Global Database, EKS, ALB, Route 53, DynamoDB, MSK, Redis, partner connectivity.
- **Targets:** No unplanned data loss; controlled traffic move with a 5-minute service interruption budget.

## 2. Detection Mechanism

Begin only after Mumbai has recovered and remained healthy for 30 minutes. Require Aurora replication lag below 1 second, zero critical alerts, healthy ALB targets in both regions, and successful synthetic transactions in both regions.

## 3. Impact Assessment

Failback is planned maintenance, not an outage. A 1,200 TPS peak may be affected if traffic is moved during peak hours. The IC schedules the change during a low-traffic window, freezes deployments, and calculates merchant impact using the current 250 TPS business-hours baseline and INR 37.5 lakh per hour planning exposure.

## 4. Immediate Response (0-2 minutes)

1. **Incident Commander (30s):** Open a controlled change record and announce a deployment freeze.
2. **SRE (20s):** Run `aws sts get-caller-identity` and verify change authorization.
3. **Communications lead (30s):** Notify engineering, management, merchants, and bank partners of the maintenance window.
4. **Application lead (30s):** Disable non-idempotent batch jobs and reduce retry concurrency.
5. **DBA (30s):** Record the current writer and replication timestamp from `aws rds describe-global-clusters --global-cluster-identifier $env:PAYSECURE_GLOBAL_CLUSTER_ID --output json`.

## 5. Diagnostic Steps (2-5 minutes)

6. **Platform engineer (20s):** Run `aws eks list-clusters --region ap-south-1 --output table` and confirm the Mumbai cluster exists.
7. **Network engineer (20s):** Run `aws elbv2 describe-target-health --target-group-arn $env:PAYSECURE_MUMBAI_TARGET_GROUP_ARN --region ap-south-1`.
8. **DBA (30s):** Confirm the target cluster with `aws rds describe-global-clusters --global-cluster-identifier $env:PAYSECURE_GLOBAL_CLUSTER_ID --query 'GlobalClusters[0].GlobalClusterMembers' --output json`.
9. **SRE (30s):** Run `kubectl --context $env:PAYSECURE_MUMBAI_KUBE_CONTEXT -n paysecure get pods` and confirm all critical replicas are ready.
10. **IC (20s):** If lag exceeds 1 second, stop and reschedule; if the target ALB or synthetic transaction fails, stop and repair Mumbai before continuing.
11. **Security engineer (20s):** Confirm KMS, Secrets Manager, WAF, and PCI controls are healthy in Mumbai.

## 6. Failover Procedure (5-15 minutes)

12. **DBA (60s):** Run `aws rds switchover-global-cluster --global-cluster-identifier $env:PAYSECURE_GLOBAL_CLUSTER_ID --target-db-cluster-identifier $env:PAYSECURE_MUMBAI_DB_CLUSTER_ID --region ap-south-1`.
13. **DBA (30s):** Poll `aws rds describe-global-clusters --global-cluster-identifier $env:PAYSECURE_GLOBAL_CLUSTER_ID --output json` until Mumbai is the sole writer; do not use a fixed sleep as proof.
14. **Application lead (30s):** Update the Mumbai application secret/configuration to the promoted writer endpoint and restart only the controlled deployment.
15. **Platform engineer (30s):** Verify with `kubectl --context $env:PAYSECURE_MUMBAI_KUBE_CONTEXT -n paysecure rollout status deployment/payment-api --timeout=30s`.
16. **Network engineer (45s):** Apply the approved primary Route 53 alias change with `aws route53 change-resource-record-sets --hosted-zone-id $env:PAYSECURE_HOSTED_ZONE_ID --change-batch file://route53-mumbai-primary.json`.
17. **Network engineer (30s):** Run `aws route53 wait resource-record-sets-changed --id $env:PAYSECURE_ROUTE53_CHANGE_ID` and verify `Resolve-DnsName api.paysecure.example`.
18. **Application lead (30s):** Execute a synthetic idempotent transaction and verify exactly one database record and one settlement event.
19. **DBA (30s):** Confirm Hyderabad has returned to a healthy secondary role and replication lag is below 1 second.
20. **IC (30s):** Re-enable controlled batch jobs, monitor for 15 minutes, and close the change only after success rate and P99 latency return to baseline.

## 7. Communication Protocol

**Engineering:** `[P1][FAILBACK][{change_id}] Mumbai failback {status}; writer {region}; lag {value}; next checkpoint {time}; bridge {link}`

**Management:** `Planned PaySecure failback is {status}. No-data-loss gate: {pass/fail}. Traffic moves to Mumbai at {time}; owner {IC}.`

**Merchants/banks:** `PaySecure planned regional maintenance is in progress. Payment processing remains available. Do not retry an unknown transaction without its original idempotency key. Next update {time}.`

**Regulators:** `Controlled failback {id}; approved window {time}; observed impact {value}; data loss {none/value}; validation evidence {link}; contact {CISO}.`

## 8. Verification and Validation

- `curl --fail-with-body https://api.paysecure.example/health/ready` returns HTTP 200.
- Mumbai critical deployments have all desired replicas available.
- Aurora shows exactly one Mumbai writer and healthy Hyderabad secondary replication.
- DNS resolves to the Mumbai ALB from independent resolvers.
- Synthetic authorization, idempotency replay, settlement, and webhook tests pass.
- No duplicate or missing transaction appears in the reconciliation query for the change window.

## 9. Rollback Procedure

If any gate fails before DNS, stop and leave Hyderabad active. If failure occurs after DNS, pause new writes, restore the last-known-good Route 53 record to Hyderabad, verify Hyderabad writer ownership, and drain Mumbai connections. Do not reverse Aurora roles until the DBA confirms synchronization and the IC approves. Record the Route 53 change ID and re-run synthetic tests after rollback.

## 10. Post-Incident Review

Within 2 business days, attach the change record, Aurora topology, replication lag, DNS INSYNC result, deployment events, synthetic evidence, and reconciliation output. Perform a five-whys review, document any client-visible impact, assign corrective actions, and update the failback drill and rollback criteria.
